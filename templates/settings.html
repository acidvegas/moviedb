How syncing works
  • Full import replaces watched and watchlist with the export's contents, so local changes are overwritten.
  • Daily sync (automatic, or Sync now) mirrors the watchlist and adds your latest 72 watched films. Older watched pages are blocked by Cloudflare unless you're logged in.
  • Letterboxd star ratings are imported only for titles you haven't rated here.
